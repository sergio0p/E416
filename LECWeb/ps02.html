The Setup

There are two agents, Antonia and Marie, two goods ($X$ and $Y$) and three periods $0$, $1$ and $2$. Prices are $p^0=(1.5,\,1.5)$, $p^1=(1,\,2)$ and $p^2=(2,\,1)$. Both have the same income, $i=30$.

Antonia
Her primary goal is to buy at least $22$ units of good $X$. If prices and income are such that she cannot afford $22$ units of $X$, she consumes only $X$. If $22\times p_X \lt i$, she buys $22$ units of $X$ and spends the remaining income $i-22\times p_X$ on good $Y$.
Marie
Her primary goal is to buy luxury goods. She uses $90\%$ of her income on the most expensive good and $10\%$ on the least expensive good. If $X$ and $Y$ have identical prices, she uses her entire income to buy the same amount of both goods.

Question 1

Antonia's baskets

Find the basket Antonia chooses in each period. Label them $A_0$, $A_1$ and $A_2$.

Enter numbers only. Fractions such as $13.5$ are fine.

Question 2

Marie's baskets

Find the basket Marie chooses in each period. Label them $M_0$, $M_1$ and $M_2$.
